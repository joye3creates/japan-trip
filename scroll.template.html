Sixteen Days, Counted

Every yen spent between Tokyo and Kyushu, 15 to 30 November 2025. Scroll to watch the country become the ledger.

Scale. Position is price on a logarithmic axis. Individual purchases run from ¥100 to ¥36,000, a range of 360 to one, and on a straight axis 89% of every mark would pile into the leftmost tenth of the page. Log spreads them. A mark twice as far right is not twice the price; read the axis.

Still missing. Thirteen of sixteen nights have no accommodation record. Steps, photographs and the handwritten notes are pending. Nothing here is invented to fill a gap.
