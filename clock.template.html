A Day at a Time

Sixteen days across Japan, laid out by the hour. Scroll to watch the country become the diary, then switch to see what any of it cost.

Times on this draft are invented, so the shape of a day is not yet real
placeholder

What is real here. Every price, every place, every journey and every night's lodging. 126 purchases from the trip book, seven confirmed bookings and two rail passes priced in yen from the original order.

What is not. The times. Twenty-four of them are inferred from words already written in the book, such as breakfast or dinner. The remaining 141 are invented so the interaction can be built before the handwritten notes are transcribed. Hover any mark and it tells you which it is.

What is missing. Anything done that cost nothing, which is most of a travelling day. Until those are in the notes, an empty morning here means nothing was bought, not that nothing happened.

Left out on purpose. Shopping and gifts. Personal, and of no use to anyone reading this as a record of travelling in Japan.
