Prefecture outlines are real, generalised to about a tenth of their surveyed points, in the manner of a printed survey sheet rather than a satellite trace. The thirteen prefectures the route touched are inked darker. Land is offset a hair from its keyline, the way a woodblock sheet misregisters.

Figures come from 158 expense lines across sixteen day sheets. Steps, photographs and the handwritten notes are still pending and are never filled with invented values.
